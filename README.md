# SwitchTax AI ⚖️
### Mid-Year Job Switch TDS Reconciler & Regulatory Compliance Automator

**Live App URL:** https://switchtax-ai-vad522rk9echq7kbvfbikb.streamlit.app/
**Problem Statement:** Track 2 - Open Innovation (AI for Legal Governance)

---

## 💡 The Problem
When salaried employees switch jobs mid-year, new employers rarely account for income earned at the previous company. This causes a silent under-deduction of tax, resulting in a massive, unexpected tax bill (a **"March TDS Shock"**) at the end of the financial year.

## 🚀 Our Solution
**SwitchTax AI** completely automates tax governance and transition compliance for salaried individuals. 
* **Smart PDF Parsing:** Extracts historical salary, PAN, TAN, and TDS directly from F&B payslips using open-source utilities.
* **Risk Monitoring Engine:** Automatically runs dual-regime checks under the latest FY 2025-26 slabs and flags deficits via a live warning banner.
* **Automated Document Workflows:** Instantly generates structurally compliant statutory forms, including **Form 12B** and a formal **Section 192(2) Declaration Letter**.

## 🛠️ Built With
* **Frontend/Backend:** Python, Streamlit
* **Data Processing & AI Logic:** streamlit , plotly, fdf2 , PDFplumber, panda, re, datetime, open-source LLM 
* **Tax Compliance Engine:** Built on top of Union Budget 2025 tax slab schemas.
